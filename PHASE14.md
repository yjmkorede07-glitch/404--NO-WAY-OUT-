# 404: NO WAY OUT — PHASE 14
## Combat + Police + Advanced Vehicle Gameplay

### Browser prototype implemented
- Five-star wanted presentation retained and connected to incident escalation.
- Crime incidents create witness/evidence data and police-pressure changes.
- Lower wanted levels can cool down when the player stays quiet.
- Higher wanted levels create dispatch units with responding/pursuit states rather than instant police teleportation.
- Nearby NPCs become more suspicious/afraid during active wanted states.
- Contextual vehicle damage.
- Individual tire damage and pop state.
- Vehicle body/engine health and speed multiplier.
- Driver panic reaction with audible prototype speech.
- Gunfire/vehicle-damage incident bridge.
- Combat statistics.
- Action panel.
- Keyboard prototype shortcuts: V = vehicle/tire action, F = firearm incident.

### Important prototype boundary
The browser version does not contain production third-person shooting, skeletal animation, projectile physics, police NavMesh pursuit, or full vehicle simulation. Those belong to the Unreal Engine implementation.

### Production target
The Phase 14 interfaces are intended to map into:
- third-person combat controller
- animation montages/state machines
- weapon inventory
- physical tire/vehicle damage
- police AI/NavMesh
- perception system
- evidence/witness system
- pursuit vehicles
- spatial audio
- cinematic arrest/escape sequences
