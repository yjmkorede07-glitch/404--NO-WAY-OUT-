# Phase 44 — Online Player System

Adds the persistent player/account layer above the existing online character and multiplayer systems.

## Included
- Player profile and presence state.
- Three character slots by default.
- Character creation, selection, and archival.
- Citizen ID per character.
- Active-character tracking.
- Last-seen/last-played and playtime/reputation persistence fields.
- Server-authoritative validation boundary.
- Explicit separation between player account and character identity.

This is the foundation for friends/social systems, player-owned progression, matchmaking/session recovery, moderation, and future cross-platform identity integration.
