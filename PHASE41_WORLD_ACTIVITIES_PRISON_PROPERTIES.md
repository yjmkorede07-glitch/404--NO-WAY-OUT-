# Phase 41 — World Activities, Custody, Properties, Construction & Maritime/Air

This phase turns the Phase 40 contracts into runtime-facing Unity components.

## Gameplay loops
- Physical banks/stores support robbery activity starts and resolution.
- Legitimate jobs originate at physical world locations.
- Properties can be purchased and extended with garage, dock, helipad or hangar where zoning allows.
- Boats and aircraft have ownership/storage runtime hooks.
- Underground spaces are physical entries and can be used for hiding/routes.
- Police custody supports station holding for 1–2 stars and early 3 stars, prison transfer for late 3 stars, 4 stars and 5 stars; bail is limited to station holding.
- Escape can begin from prison or station holding. Escaped players enter the fugitive state; the special fugitive period is 10 in-game days, while ordinary wanted rules still apply to new crimes.

## No fake completion
These are runtime contracts, not final art. Final 3D facilities, characters, animations, vehicles, audio and authored scenes remain production assets.
