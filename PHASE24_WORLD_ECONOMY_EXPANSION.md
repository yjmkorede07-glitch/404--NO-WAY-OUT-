# 404: NO WAY OUT — Phase 24 World Economy Expansion

This phase locks the game as an open-world simulation rather than a mission list.

## Core decision
Story and Online both support free-roam interaction with the world. Authored missions remain important, but the player can independently visit banks, stores, businesses, properties, dealerships, construction offices and other locations and initiate activities.

## Inspired-by, not copied
The design takes broad structural lessons from GTA V/GTA Online: physical properties, purchasable businesses, vehicles, freemode activities, dynamic economy and websites that are functional parts of the world. It does not copy Rockstar code, names, UI, characters, maps or assets.

## Story mode
The story campaign can use:
- property ownership
- vehicle ownership
- business ownership
- stock trading through the fictional Veyron Exchange
- BlackNet fictional marketplace
- construction and home upgrades
- bank/store freemode activities

Story missions may react to these systems. Example: a bank robbery in free roam can increase security or alter later mission conditions at that bank.

## Online mode
Online uses separate persistent ownership and server-authoritative economy ledgers. Players can build a criminal/legitimate portfolio over time.

## Construction
Veyron Build & Design is an in-world company with a website, phone contact and physical office. The player can choose an owned house to upgrade or select an approved buildable plot. Construction progresses with in-game time and changes the physical world state in stages.

## World scale
The world expands beyond the original eight districts into mountain, forest, rural, lake and alpine regions. Snowline Resort is a major destination with winter gameplay. The map should be large because it contains meaningful regions and travel routes, not because of empty filler.
