# 404: NO WAY OUT — Online First Mission: First Day in Veyron

## Locked rule
Online players do not spawn with a house, car, business, police privileges, or a complete inventory. After character creation, the server grants **10,000,000 VCR** to the player's bank balance as starter capital. The player must decide how to live and spend that money.

## Flow
1. Create Online character.
2. Choose Veyron world/instance preference.
3. Choose life path: Legitimate, Criminal, or Law Enforcement.
4. Law Enforcement is **application-only** and requires human/admin acceptance. Normal NPC police remain active regardless of player applications.
5. System recommends districts that fit the selected life.
6. Player selects a home district/property and buys a starter house.
7. Player visits the civic registry and obtains a Citizen ID.
8. Player buys a starter vehicle.
9. First Day in Veyron completes and free-roam opens.
10. Player can then pursue authored Online missions, freemode crime, legitimate work, properties, businesses, construction, markets, social activity, and other systems.

## Starter money rules
- 10,000,000 VCR is granted once after character creation.
- It is bank money, not a free asset bundle.
- No automatic house, business, vehicle collection, or weapons bundle is granted.
- Starter purchases are ordinary server-validated transactions.
- Recreating a character does not mint another grant on the same account.

## Life paths
### Legitimate
Examples: employee, trader, mechanic, construction, transport, business owner.

### Criminal
Examples: street crew, heist operator, smuggler, vehicle thief, fixer, cyber broker.

### Law Enforcement
Application required. A human/admin reviews the application. Until accepted, the player has no police-player privileges. NPC police continue to patrol and respond to crime normally.

## Citizen ID
Citizen ID is a persistent Online identity record. It is used for banking, vehicle/property registration, contracts and future restricted services. The ID is obtained in-world at a civic registry rather than appearing automatically in the player's inventory.

## Physical-world requirement
The onboarding mission intentionally makes the player travel through Veyron. Web/phone interfaces are convenience layers; they do not replace the physical bank, dealership, civic registry, property and other world locations.
