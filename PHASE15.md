# 404: NO WAY OUT — PHASE 15
## Interiors + Properties + Personal Life

### Implemented in browser prototype
- Building/interior registry with floor data.
- Cole family house interior access.
- Veyron Grand Hotel booking state and room access.
- Multi-story property catalog.
- Property purchasing and persistent ownership.
- Property upgrades and garage/storage expansion.
- Floor switching.
- Hotel room booking and room access.
- Personal-life meters: hunger, hygiene, energy, social.
- Sleep/rest, shower, eating, phone, laptop, wardrobe and storage actions.
- Cash storage/withdrawal.
- Wardrobe outfit state.
- Interior and property UI.

### Example supported property
Iron District Loft:
- Floor 1: living room, kitchen, garage
- Floor 2: bedroom, bathroom, office
- Floor 3: gym, private room, terrace

### Production 3D boundary

- fully modeled interiors
- stairs/elevators
- third-person navigation
- animation
- furniture interaction
- persistent world streaming
- vehicle garage spawning
- realistic hotel reception/elevator flow
- cinematic social/relationship scenes
