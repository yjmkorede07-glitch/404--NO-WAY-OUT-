# Phase 10 — The Final Chapter

## Four major endings
1. The Escape — hopeful-bitter.
2. The Crown — dark-triumphant.
3. The Sacrifice — bittersweet.
4. No Way Out — dark.

Each ending has its own final sequence and post-game consequences. Smaller variations (8–12) can change survivors, relationships, faction control, properties and available post-game content.

## Mission score
Every mission can produce a 0–100 score:
- Critical objectives: 50
- Optional objectives: 20
- Time: 10
- Damage/survival: 10
- Stealth/precision: 10

Score bands:
90–100 Exceptional
75–89 Excellent
60–74 Passed
40–59 Barely Passed
0–39 Failed

Critical objective failure can immediately fail a mission regardless of optional performance. Optional objectives do not normally cause mission failure.

## Post-game free roam
After the finale, Story Mode remains playable. The city state, NPC relationships, faction behavior, businesses, properties and available activities can reflect the selected ending.

## Playable cast
Darius, Malik and Amara remain the three playable/switchable protagonists. The added major story characters are NPCs, not playable protagonists.
