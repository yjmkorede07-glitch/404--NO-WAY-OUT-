# Phase 8 — Economy, Properties & Personal Life

The economy is server-authoritative and persistent.

- Cash is stored in the account profile.
- Property purchases and upgrades are validated and saved by the backend.
- Businesses persist level, balance and active state.
- Vehicles can be stored at owned properties.
- Economy state is restored after login.
- Mission rewards should be granted by the server in later mission integration.

Catalog: safehouse, apartment, house, garage, warehouse, dock, Corner Market, Auto Workshop.
