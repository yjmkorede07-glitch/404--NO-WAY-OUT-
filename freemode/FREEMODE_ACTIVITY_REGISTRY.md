# Freemode activity registry — first production pass

## Bank robberies
- `bank_veyron_central` — Veyron Bank — Tier 3 — $8,000–$22,000
- `bank_central_reserve` — Central Reserve Bank — Tier 5 — $25,000–$70,000
- `bank_east_market` — East Market Credit Union — Tier 2 — $4,000–$12,000
- `bank_port_finance` — Port Finance — Tier 3 — $7,000–$18,000

These are gameplay design ranges, not real-world financial instructions.

## Future activity expansion
The same system will cover stores, armored transports, cash pickups, vehicle theft, territory events and police intercepts. Each activity gets a data definition instead of a hard-coded mission.
