# 404 ONLINE ROLEPLAY WORLD — Phase 25

## Goal
Create an RP-friendly shared-world layer inspired by the *concept* of choosing a server/world and establishing an identity, without copying FiveM/Rockstar code or assets.

## Character entry
1. Choose Veyron world/shard.
2. Create player identity.
3. Customize appearance when final character creator assets are available.
4. Choose home district.
5. Spawn in that district.
6. Visit a Civic Center/Registry Office and obtain a physical Veyron Citizen ID.
7. Unlock normal banking, property contracts, business registration and vehicle registration.

## World choice
- Veyron One: balanced population/event mix.
- Veyron Two: social/event-heavy mix.
- Veyron Three: higher criminal-event mix.

World choice is not a permanent content lock. Players can later transfer world through a controlled migration flow when the production backend supports it.

## RP systems
- physical ID and identity records
- jobs/business ownership
- property ownership
- player-to-player bank transfers
- cash and bank accounts
- businesses and contracts
- physical stores/banks/ATMs
- freemode crimes and police response
- construction projects
- player groups/parties
- optional voice proximity architecture

## Important boundary
The game does not need a public server browser full of arbitrary community code. We control the official Veyron worlds. A later dedicated-server/community-server layer can be added only if it is technically and legally appropriate.
