# ONLINE ENTRY FLOW — LOCKED

## First-time player
1. ONLINE
2. Choose Veyron world: Veyron One / Two / Three.
3. Create player: display name + appearance + home district.
4. Confirm character.
5. Enter shared world.
6. Spawn at selected home district.
7. Receive a temporary digital identity card.
8. Visit a Civic Center / Municipal Office / Registry to activate the physical Veyron Citizen ID.
9. Banking, property contracts, business registration and vehicle registration become available.

## Returning player
1. ONLINE
2. Continue character.
3. Server restores identity, world, property, businesses, vehicles, balances and progression.
4. Rejoin the selected world or use the controlled world-transfer flow.

## World selection philosophy
The three official worlds are different server instances/event mixes, not separate games. Content is shared across all worlds. A future migration flow can move an account between worlds with cooldown and anti-duplication checks.
