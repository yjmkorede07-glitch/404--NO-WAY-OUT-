# Phase 51 — Health, Backpack & Quick-Use Wheel

Health does not passively regenerate during normal gameplay. Players recover by consuming physical items: snacks provide small recovery, bandages provide medium recovery, and medical kits provide larger recovery. The existing combat system remains authoritative for health.

A persistent backpack inventory is added for carrying consumables, weapons and utility items. The weapon wheel is not replaced: it becomes a broader quick-use wheel, with weapon, medical, food and utility categories. The backpack is the full inventory; the wheel is the fast-access layer.

No universal E interaction is introduced.
